## Chosen direction

The approved mark stacks the words `Sites`, `By`, and `Leon` on separate centered lines. It is larger and vertically centered, with a handwritten signature character and no camera, aperture, or photography-business symbol.

The website recreates the mark with locally hosted type so it remains crisp, responsive, accessible, and easy to replace with a future exported vector. Canva remains the visual reference.

## Export record

No raster logo export is required for Phase 1. The live wordmark is rendered as text and the favicon is a separate original SVG. Any future Canva export must be optimized before entering `public/`.
